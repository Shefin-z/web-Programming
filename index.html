How does a parking reservation work?

Choose a location and time, confirm your vehicle, and pay securely. We will assign your space and provide a one-time code for entry.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Most reservations can be changed or cancelled free of charge until 30 minutes before the scheduled arrival time.

What happens if I stay longer than planned?

You can extend an active reservation from your driver dashboard when the space remains available. The additional time uses the current hourly rate.

How do I report a parking issue?

Open your active reservation to message the parking manager or submit an issue report with the location, category, and supporting details.
